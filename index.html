About

Hillel Bazak is an artist and illustrator working in traditional and digital media. His work focuses on narrative composition, texture, and evocative color. He has produced fine art paintings, editorial illustrations, and collaborative projects for clients and exhibitions.

Mediums: oil, acrylic, colored pencil, mixed media. Available for commissions, exhibitions, and editorial work.
