About

Hillel Bazak is an artist and illustrator working between painting, illustration, and storytelling. His practice is driven by visual narratives, tactile surfaces, and a love for expressive color and mark-making.

Through painting and illustration, he creates work that blends character, atmosphere, and emotional core. The portfolio below highlights paintings, drawings, projects, and events.
