About

Hillel Bazak is a professional painter and illustrator. Bazak combines techniques learned from various masters and artists, and his inspirations blend classical, archaeological, and modern motifs. His work is characterized by meticulous detail and a deep attention to symbolism, expression, and historical context. Bazak is an expert in creating comprehensive, immersive visual worlds and a unique, original visual language. Alongside his mastery of traditional techniques, he embraces modern creative technologies—including AI and rendering tools—while keeping his signature rich and original style

Through painting and illustration, he creates work that blends character, atmosphere, and emotional core. The portfolio below highlights paintings, drawings, projects, and events.
